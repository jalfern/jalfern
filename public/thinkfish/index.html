About LiveArt

🐟 LiveArt

ThinkFish, 1997–1999. This is the original LiveArt rendering engine — the C++ written then — compiled to WebAssembly. Every frame is drawn by the engine's own EPS writer.

Drag to rotate · right-drag or scroll to zoom · pick a LiveStyle on the right.

Models: the editor's 1998 sample, and 3D Studio files from Viewpoint Datalabs' DataShop catalog.
