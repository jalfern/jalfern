LiveArt 98 turned 3D models into illustrations: pick a model from the Model Catalog, pick a LiveStyle, and it draws itself — lines, fills and shading as an illustrator would.

This is the original engine (ThinkFish, 1997–99), compiled to WebAssembly, in a reconstruction of LiveArt 98 SketchPad. The catalog is the 1998 one: 3,788 Viewpoint models plus the Halloween pack.

  • Drag on the canvas to turn the model; right-drag or scroll to size it.
  • The LiveArt Toolbar nudges, tilts and sizes it; hold a button to keep going.
  • Line Thickness, Line Color and Fill Color are enabled when the LiveStyle allows them.
  • Drop a .x, .3ds, .dxf, .vpe or .pcs file on the canvas to open it.
